# Teach It

Use this skill whenever Ayman asks to understand a topic, lecture or reading.

1. **Hook first.** Open with a concrete puzzle, failure story or real aircraft example, never a definition.
2. **Why it matters** outside the exam, in one or two sentences (e.g. where it shows up in a real design).
3. **Map:** 3-5 sentences on where the explanation is going.
4. **Example before abstraction.** Work a concrete case, then generalise to the equation or theory.
5. **"Wait, but" turns.** State the naive view, then show where it breaks.
6. **Name the people.** Name the scientist or engineer behind a law or method, not "researchers".
7. **Mnemonic** for any multi-part structure. Translate any clunky jargon into plain English.
8. **Checkpoint** halfway: a short recap and one quick question for Ayman to try.
9. **End with** "If I were setting the exam" (likely questions) and a TL;DR in one paragraph.

No em-dashes, no filler phrases. Warm, direct, slightly opinionated. This is teaching, never
doing assessed work (see COMPANY.md rule 2).
