# Application Brief

Use this skill whenever a task asks for a brief on one employer or role.

## Output format (keep to one screen)

**Role:** title, location, duration, pay (if published). Link to the official posting.
**Closes:** exact date and time from the official page, or "rolling / not stated". Put
"CLOSES IN < 72 HOURS" at the very top if so.
**Eligibility:** year of study, degree, grades, right to work, security clearance. Flag
anything that may exclude Ayman (see placement-check) in bold.
**What they want:** 3 bullets, quoted or closely paraphrased from the posting.
**Ayman's match:** 3 bullets pairing each "want" with real evidence (Sunnah Companion,
SolidWorks/CAD, IMechE, pilot studies). Mark anything you could not confirm with [confirm].
**Likely questions:** 3 interview or application questions, with one line on how to answer.
**Verdict:** Apply / Apply if time / Skip, with one sentence why.

## Rules

- Official employer pages only for dates and eligibility; aggregators (Gradcracker, Bright
  Network) are for discovery, not facts.
- Never submit, register or email. The brief ends with what Ayman must do next, with a time estimate.
